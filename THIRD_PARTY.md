# Third-party material

The MIT License in this artifact applies to CyBER, the experiment-processing
scripts, the master result data, and the generated experiment artifacts created
by the CyBER authors.

The files under `benchmarks/BBM/` are third-party scientific models collected
by the Biodivine Boolean Models repository.  They are included to identify and
reproduce the evaluated instances, but are not relicensed by this artifact.
Consult the BBM repository and the original model sources for their applicable
terms and attribution requirements.

CyBER depends on `biodivine-lib-bdd` and `num-bigint`; their source code is not
vendored here.  Cargo retrieves them under their respective upstream licenses.
